Are you building a Rube Goldberg machine with your CRM?

At this point I think it’s safe to say that we have been over engineering our IT departments since the onset of Cloud Computing.

At least before the firewall port was reluctantly opened up by IT, most companies could at least PRETEND to have a well thought out system architecture.

Where is the source of truth for data in your company? I’m betting in this day and age you either don’t have one or don’t know who knows the answer to that question. I have met exactly zero companies that have been able to architect and implement an effective data architecture strategy for their corporate data set in the age of cloud computing.

Service oriented architecture anyone? Master data management? Do these concepts still apply in 2024? Or are we just throwing all of this nonsense data into a “data lake” and hoping AI will make sense out of it someday…

I honestly can’t remember the last time I had a client who wanted to talk about software services, source of truth, enterprise architecture, software design patterns, etc. Basically it is usually just a frenzy of integrating cloud service providers together as quickly and “easily” as possible. Most CRM and marketing investments are driven by executive stakeholders with one and only one goal: increase attributable revenue as fast as possible.

The problem with this goal is that the decentralized data architecture in most enterprises makes “fast as possible” seem like you are implementing SAP in the 1990s. Its going to take time. Its going to take money. Its going to take a LOT of consulting.

Have you actually looked at the Marketing Technology Landscape on martechmap.com? I think its safe to say we maybe over-solutioning…

We are no longer building spaghetti code. We are building spaghetti architectures. And the longer this continues the worse the problem will become. As more decentralized technology arrives to “disrupt” IT, more data and processes will move to additional cloud service providers. The problem is going to get worse — not better. Our technical innovation in the age of cloud and AI is crippling our ability to build robust, reliable, and “simple” solutions within a reasonable time-to-market that actually can impact the bottom line.

If you are a CEO or CIO who enjoys spending millions of dollars trying to integrate hundreds of disparate, disconnected data sources together, this article is not for you.

If you are a CEO or CIO who is heavily invested in Salesforce.com (yes just the boring and un-shiny penny of the primary force.com platform) and trying to figure out how to simplify this complex nightmare you found yourself in? Read on.

One of my biggest challenges in approaching new customers is the conscientious and humbling learning curve necessary to a) realize you have found yourself in a technology nightmare b) understand it is only getting worse and c) understand there is an alternative approach.

We built Reactforce as a response to the nonsensical technology landscape that is emerging out of the decentralized cloud landscape. We are actually taking the opposite approach of Salesforce, the Big-4, and most large companies that I have been burning the candle on both ends to save. My goal in this article is to help those C-level executives become aware of what is REALLY going on, and better yet, what can be done about it.

So what is going on?

Here is my opinionated take on the IT landscape today.

  1. Data is everywhere.
  2. True Data Security is almost non-existent at this point.
  3. Business are trying to mitigate this reality with privacy regulations and processes to mitigate the impacts of a breach.
  4. IT Departments are trying to stop the bleeding with basic bandaids such as SSO (which never stopped a good hacker from what I can tell).
  5. IT departments cannot keep up with the demands of business stakeholders who DON’T CARE about the system architecture. They care about results that will boost their own career trajectory at the expense of responsible IT practices.
  6. Incumbent technology and consulting providers are moving us towards “big data” and AI to attempt to retroactively make sense of your operational data which includes a LOT of money in software licensing and much more money in implementation and support of those software licenses.
  7. Companies abstract this complexity underneath their next large capital investment, hoping this spaghetti mess will just “go-away” after the arrival of the next big thing.

Keep it simple stupid

Have you ever considered what we actually do here?

  1. Create a website for someone to request some information
  2. Uncontrollably spam them in the hopes they will convert to an opportunity
  3. Uncontrollably prompt and track every interaction with them in the hopes that the opportunity will convert into revenue.

Do we really need 10, 50, even a hundred systems to do this? Do we need even more than one? I don’t want to necessarily assume that today’s complexity is based upon nefarious purposes, rather we can all hope that this is an accidental and evolutionary response to sheep being sheep.

Back to Basics

Alternative Architecture

We use Reactforce solutions to build micro (and in some case macro) solutions that are hosted on Salesforce.com. This allows us to build solutions directly on top of the best data source, which for most companies has become their Salesforce.com CRM database.

Reactforce sites can have the complexity and sophistication of almost any modern day tech solution, however your technology is built directly on top of your data and therefore has ZERO data integration requirement. We build directly on top of your existing data. This removes the need to move data via Mulesoft, Jitterbit, or any other middleware platform to some form of data warehouse or operational datastore. The licensing and operational savings from removing this technical hurdle is hard to quantify, but it is $$$.

The Reactforce application also leverages your existing investments with Salesforce.com to provide a scalable hosting solution for your enterprise systems with no more effort than deploying a single visualforce page.

We also provide an API management solution native to Salesforce.com that allows you to restart your services oriented architecture initiative with a Fit-for-Purpose solution that is 100% native, secure, and robust. This allows the front-end solution to be built with API contracts and even better, commodity resources. You would not believe how fast an enterprise solution can come to life with Reactforce on top of Salesforce.com with a group of React developers that have never worked with Salesforce.com before.
