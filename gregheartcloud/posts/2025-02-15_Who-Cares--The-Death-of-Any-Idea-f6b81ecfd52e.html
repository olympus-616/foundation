Who Cares? The Death of Any Idea

Artist’s Musings

It’s been almost 20 years since I was given the lesson.

I didn’t listen.

Not for 20 years.

I’ve built app upon app.
I’ve helped company upon company.
I’ve seen success, failure, growth, collapse, pivots, acquisitions, betrayals, and rebirths.

And through all of it, the same two words have haunted every idea, every venture, every late-night brainstorm session that never saw the light of day:

WHO CARES?

The Question That Kills Every Idea

The beginning of my entrepreneurial future was more than 20 years ago (which is a terrifying thought that makes me feel somewhere between the old woman in Donnie Darko and the handsome version of Benjamin Button… I’ll let you know how that works out in a few years).

I went to startup meetups in Boulder, CO, desperate to be part of the energy, desperate to believe in something larger than my own cubicle.

I don’t remember much from those nights.
The speakers, the presentations, the endless hype about “game-changing” tech.

None of it mattered.

Because one man, in a room full of idealists, delivered the only truth that ever stuck.

Two words.

Two words that can kill almost anything.

Two words that, to this day, still determine the fate of every business, every movement, every dream ever conceived:

WHO CARES?

The Age of Apathy

These two words destroy and create in seconds.

Because let’s be honest: most people don’t care.

And in the world we’ve built today, it seems like apathy is the dominant force.

As tech makes life “simpler,” we’ve only become more co-dependent.

As our social media feeds confuse politics and celebrity gossip in the same algorithm, we are conditioned to care about everything and nothing at the same time.

So much noise.
So much attention warfare.
And in the end, the only currency that matters isn’t money — it’s caring.

Because caring is energy.
Caring is belief.
Caring is manifestation.

Entire companies rise and fall on the who cares? of their next opportunity.

Entire movements die in obscurity because not enough people cared at the right time.

So after more than:

  • 30 years of building software,
  • 20 years of being an entrepreneur,
  • 10 years of being a certified technical architect,
  • 3+ years of studying metaphysics, spirituality, philosophy, and consciousness…

I am finally ready to answer the question.

Who Cares?

I do.

Because I know the hidden secret.

I know that what I care about will manifest into my reality.

What I Don’t Care About

  • Politics? No.
  • Nightly news? No.
  • Healthcare commercials being projected into society 24/7? No.
  • AI replacing everyone’s jobs? No.

You know why?

Because I know who DOES care — and it’s the people you feed.

And when you are feeding someone, you can say anything to them.
They will listen.
Because their survival is on the line.

And I know that i will do anything to feed the people that I care about.

The World Is Starving

The entire system is starving.

Because people don’t know where their next meal comes from — and I’m not just talking about food.

We live:

  • Paycheck to paycheck.
  • Deal to deal.
  • Stock price to stock price.
  • Social media post to social media post.

And none of it matters… except for two things:

1️⃣ Can I breathe?
2️⃣ Where is my next meal coming from?

Yet, while the world barely survives, we watch as the elite brag themselves into some form of dystopian oligarchy.

This is not the future I choose to care about.

So After 20 Years… I Return to the Same Question

As I launch what is truly my magnum opus, I return to the same question I first heard in that Boulder startup meetup so many years ago.

Who Cares?

  • Do I need an investor to care, so I can prove I’m not just robbing enterprises of $100M like every other tech giant?
  • Do I need a social media following to care, so I can pay some trendy celebrity to say, “Try Olympus-Grid! It’s the best!”?
  • Do I need anyone other than my own experience to validate my path?

No.

Because after everything I’ve learned, I now know the answer.

Who Cares?

Those You Feed.

That’s it.

That’s the answer to everything.

If you feed people, they will follow you anywhere.

If you don’t feed them, they will never care about a single word that comes out of your mouth.

So as I move forward, as I build the foundation of the future, I have one mission:

Feed the right people.

Because they will care.

And in the end, they are the only ones that matter.

Final Thoughts

This article marks another entry in the Artist’s Musings series — my raw, unfiltered thoughts on entrepreneurship, technology, and reality itself.

If you’re reading this, ask yourself:

Who do you feed?
And if the answer is no one, then ask yourself:

Who cares?
