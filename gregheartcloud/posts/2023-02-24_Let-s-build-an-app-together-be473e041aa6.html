Let’s build an app together

I’ve been a salesforce.com certified technical architect for 10 years. I used to be an advanced developer as well until I forgot to renew my certs (whoops!) one time… so maybe I’ll go back through the certification journey again someday.

Either way, I’ve been an independent software architect for a long time and that has taken me through a ton of unique and challenging projects. I have been so busy with my consulting and software development that I stopped writing my blog, just as things were starting to take off. I even had some brief notoriety in the Salesforce eco system but there is just something about billable hours that keeps me very VERY busy.

In my career I have built numerous Salesforce.com ISV apps, consulted for a wide range of start-ups and Fortune 100s, and taken a handfull of stints as CTO and and tech founder for multiple companies. It’s not exactly Social Network but its the best that I’ve been able to come up with so far.

I finally came to a point in my career that I am ready to share some of what I have learned in 20+ years of building apps. I actually started writing code when I was 12 (Pascal anyone?) and although I’m not going to blow anyone’s mind with my use of abstract classes, I do think I have something that is worth sharing.

This blog is going to cover my journey. It is going to be awesome because I’ve already been using these solutions for years and I’m really excited to show others how to build robust and secure apps fast and cheap.

Obviously there is a lot of noise in the Salesforce community and in no way do I think my tiny little blog is going to change the world… however it has radically changed my life and all of the clients and companies that I have worked with. So who knows… maybe it will.

So what am I going to build? A lot actually… I’ve been saving up ideas for along time and its time to just get some of this stuff out of my head and into the world. I have no idea if anyone will ever use any of this other than me, nor do I care. But lets just pretend that you stumble across this blog in your own journey as a software architect. You may find some of these approaches to be very innovative. Maybe somewhat controversial approaches actually.

This blog will at least cover 3 projects. Maybe more depending on what happens after that.

Project #1 — My first project is a SFDX plug-in command line tool for helping to bootstrap react development on Salesforce. There are others out there using React on Salesforce already… but I have an end-to-end solution that completely flies in the face of standard Salesforce.com development. It actually puts React as front and center of the development model and provides scaffolding and tooling for the entire lifecycle of react development. Its awesome and I can’t wait to show it and explain it.

Project #2 — The next project will is an Salesforce ISV application that is intended to be a reusable library to assist in the creation of any apps, including any react apps that will be used. It has all sorts of reusable frameworks and tools that focus on the non-functional requirements that I’ve always had on any important project. I will cover how I built the app, what it does, and how to use it.

Project #3 — I will be building a freemium application for Salesforce using the two projects above. I will show you how using a combination of the react plugin and the salesforce utility library how easy it is to build a state of the art Salesforce application… including how to get through the security review process (which I will have to do 2 times).

Its going to be awesome. I’ve always been scared someone was going to do all this first… and now I’m going to give it all away. I really just want to see it all exist. And who knows… maybe it will change the world in my own small and innovative way.

Let’s get started…
