The Odyssey of {{FirstName__c}}

One of my favorite stories is Homer’s Odyssey.

I find the characterization of the main character Odysseus to be a compelling and vastly underrated journey through a crazy world that probably didn’t honestly feel that real to him… but he just kept trying to get home to his family and eventually he did.

Singular vision. Integrity. Focus. Determination. Strength. Intelligence. Resilience. Strategy. Courage. Odysseus rocks.

Sometimes I wonder if Homer was really just writing a journal about his own journey to Troy and back. How else could you come up with such incredible stories?

One of my favorite parts of the book… (there are so many) is when Odysseus declares his name to the one-eyed monster who had ship wrecked his crew:

I am nobody.

Even though this blog’s intention was technical, it has tapped into some level of my storytelling and philosophy and so I have decided it would be best if I just focus on launching the company for now. I can already feel the narcissistic dopamine of excitement that happens when someone clicks the like button and wants to be part of the Reactforce bandwagon. Trust me. I get it… It is awesome and for the most part work is boring.

But I think before I finalize my marketing and brand strategy, which is what this blog has recently become, I should finish the story about the launch of this technology and my personal intentions for its successful realization.

Who am I? I am nobody.

I am basically just a young kid in an adult body and a busy day job who loves legos and found a way to get paid to build legos for his whole life. And although most of my legos happen to be invisible… after 3 years in disciplined dedication the product vision is finally being realized simultaneously as the demand AND the support for the demand has magically materialized. Almost like it was meant to be.

So rather than making this story about myself I would like to hand the pen across the dimensions of space and time and let YOU be the writer of the next chapter. I am nobody. Who are you? And what can YOU do given the opportunity?

How will YOU use this technology? What will YOUR story be? Given this software was inspired by just one project worth $100M I have a feeling the potential of opportunities for cost savings or revenue growth is quite kinetic. Can you feel it? What if you could build something that other companies built for $100m with a fraction of the time, resources, or budget. What impact could YOU make?

When I look back on my life I hope that the vision and the hard work that has gone into this platform will actually be put towards what it should… the RIGHT thing.

If you ever find me as a tech billionaire and launching myself into space in my fancy spaceship I hope you will approach me and hand me a copy of Homer’s Odyssey (and maybe Jesus’s sermon on the mount while you’re at it) and suggest that millions and billions of dollars of wealth and power should POSSIBLY be reorganized in this current day and age. And i love capitalism for the record. I just hate selfishness.

Do we REALLY need more wealth in tech? Better AI? Honestly not really. I don’t know if the robots will be able to optimize the door dash and uber rides much more effectively than a few other React applications have already done. I digress.

My challenge for anyone who reads this…

Imagine you are the hero of your own story. Imagine that you had the power to change the world. For the better. (Or the worse for that matter but I try to not let my head go there). What would YOU do? Would you find a comfortable life? Would you take the easy road? Would you let injustice walk by? Would you talk to the Samaritan? Would you overturn the tables?

With all of my passion and stamina I have attempted to solve an injustice I saw in my life. Even if I fail… at least I will stand proud after my next failed start-up.

But this time I won’t fail. Because this story is not about me this time. It is about you.

“To believe in something, and not to live it, is dishonest.” — Mahatma Gandhi

So I ask you one more time…

What would YOU do if you could change the world?

Well I have one suggestion. Go do it. You will never find satisfaction in anything else and I have looked far and wide.

— The anonymous writer.
