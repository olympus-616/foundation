Introducing Olympus-Grid: Redefining Enterprise Architecture

After years of relentless effort, countless design iterations, and yet another wildly successful beta, I’m thrilled to announce that Olympus-Grid has officially been submitted to the Salesforce AppExchange for public availability. This marks a significant milestone in my 30-year programming journey, including 15 years as a Salesforce Certified Technical Architect. Olympus-Grid isn’t just another enterprise tool — it’s the culmination of decades of experience, innovation, and a passion for solving the most complex challenges in enterprise software.

What is Olympus-Grid?

Olympus-Grid is more than a product; it’s a reimagining of how enterprise applications are built, deployed, and scaled. At its core, Olympus-Grid delivers a full-stack enterprise architecture platform designed to:

• Empower Developers with reusable libraries, prebuilt APIs, and rapid development tools.

• Delight Businesses with secure, scalable, and cost-effective solutions that accelerate time to market.

• Simplify Complexity by integrating everything into a single managed package or a managed service, eliminating technical overhead.

Whether you’re a start-up looking for enterprise-grade reliability or an established enterprise seeking agility, Olympus-Grid is designed to meet you where you are and take you where you want to go.

A Proven History of Success

This isn’t my first foray into building innovative software. My career spans three decades, with a proven track record of delivering large, complex systems that work. Notable highlights include:

• Developing an AppExchange app that became the engine for one of Silicon Valley’s most successful marketing analytics companies.

• Designing a service management platform that was acquired by one of the world’s top five call centers.

Olympus-Grid is the next chapter in this journey, bringing together all the lessons, successes, and challenges I’ve faced along the way.

Key Features of Olympus-Grid

Olympus-Grid delivers a robust set of features to meet the needs of modern enterprises:

1. Proteus Objects

Abstract data integration that makes it easy to move between Salesforce and non-Salesforce platforms.

2. Hermes Messaging

A unified communication stack for managing APIs, email, SMS, and more.

3. Iris Enterprise Portal

A fully compliant ReactJS portal for building rich user experiences on Salesforce or hosted solutions like Netlify.

4. Chronos BPM

A business process management engine to streamline and automate workflows.

5. Orion Threads

Manage asynchronous job workloads with detailed logs and alerts.

6. Dynamic Forms Engine

Quickly create and deploy forms directly in production.

7. HermesCLI

A powerful command-line interface to automate development, manage deployments, and access Olympus-Grid’s full capabilities.

Flexible Pricing to Meet Your Needs

Olympus-Grid is designed to be accessible to businesses of all sizes, with various key tiers depending on where you are in your journey:

Essentials (Free)

Start-Up ($499/company/month)

Enterprise (Contact for Pricing)
