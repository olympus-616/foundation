How to convert from consultant to license software provider…

I am not going to lie… I love this blog so far and it has by far exceeded my expectations as to how to focus myself on my career and goals and to pursue and build my own career interests.

As a software consultant of over 25 years I would say that the days of answering the same questions over and over and creating the same solutions over and over is starting to feel as easy as using a lightsaber to fight droids.

I really at this point have decided that I am most likely not going to make a very good employee with out a LOT of equity. I’m sorry to be gut wrenchingly honest, but it is true.

In my last stint as a CTO I was asked by the CEO to help him increase the value of his company from $10M to $100M.

“Yes I can do that” I said… but first of all i need a budget of $9M (I will return 10x easy) and also I would like to be paid in equity in increasing scale the closer we hit the goal. In other words… if I spend my days, evenings, weekends, kids soccer games, vacations, thoughts while walking the dog, irritating thoughts about invisible software while trying to focus on a date with my wife… waking up in the middle of the night when something doesn’t work, staying after work until the project is completed without error… well honestly I would like to be rewarded someday for the stress that I have put on my personal relationships and physical health. It would be a LOT easier for me to have decided to just take the easy consulting path and live a very comfortable life serving a small number of very loyal customers. But the impact would remain as small as the number of business relationships that I can personally maintain. The business is never scalable more than my ability to sleep even less than i usually do at night.

There must be a better way.

For I only know of one honest form of alchemy on this planet and it is the radical transformation from a non-existent or inefficient business process into millions of dollars of value or savings using nothing more than my keyboard and my time. And knowing the scope of these transformations and the budgets of these projects… I guess I have some kind of tick inside of me that wants to convert my time into radical transformation, regardless of how big the transformation may be.

One of my last start-ups was in the social media space. I couldn’t even get people to look at it, let alone take it from me, and actually I couldn’t even PAY people to use it.

Dang that was a bad business.

But the technology was amazing. We created a serverless social engine search engine that was personalized for the influencer and then able to be searched by their followers… all from their link in bio. It was incredible. Apply the social network effect and then you could search personalized search engines indexed only by the influencers that you trust in certain categories. Absolutely incredible.

No one cared. (Btw when AI is fully in control you will know it was smearch and search my social that could have saved us all lol)

I am about to go off the blog for a bit (I think). Although I am getting better at adding content in stream of consciousness so it helps me to not have to do a lot of editing. That being said sorry for some of the impulsivity or the 100% honest and genuine voice that I try to use at all times. It helps my clients to trust me to be honest and somewhat dismissive of unnecessary bloat and over-engineering of many of today’s technology implementations.

Since I have started writing about the availability of reactforce I have had a number of accelerated conversations with existing and new prospects. Everyone loves the concept, and they love the demo even more. I basically need to spin up a large delivery organization if I am going to be able to meet the demand that I’ve already generated with nothing more than a few blog articles on medium. Just wait until I throw some ad spend on it and use our attribution engine to efficiently optimize ad spend on the fly… this thing is going to explode.

And I’m not ready.

So thats my job now. Prepare to scale this thing. The tech is done (never really done but good enough) and the market has validated the concepts immediately, and much more positively than I could have honestly imagined which has been very exciting.

In the next year of my life, my company, this blog, etc I expect to see the following incredible success with the launch of the Reactforce tech stack and related business opportunities:

  1. Launch of a company help-desk ticketing system for azure based logins to create and manage cases and support tickets using salesforce cases
  2. Replacement of a companies event scheduling portal
  3. Continued development and beta launch of the enterprise portal, providing an incredible jump start for any of your portal needs as well as a container to manage all your react apps running 100% natively from the Salesforce.com platform (no heroku, node.js, marketing cloud, or mulesoft required)
  4. Launch of our healthcare development platform as a service (current working title is Reactforce CRM but i figure that is just a working title) for quickly building, deploying, and managing HIPAA compliant portals, web apps, and native apps with a few clicks of a button.
  5. The development backbone of a new financial services and fund-raising portal.
  6. Managed services development and support for a companies hospital registration system
  7. A new business using Reactforce tech to build and integrate behavior health EMRs to our marketing automation solution. (Behavior health platform in a box)
  8. Launch of our managed services reactforce platform — you only provide the wireframes and a credit card. We are using this as the backbone of an existing healthcare application to connect caregivers with their family and eventually patients for monitoring medicine and heath stats and progress remotely with smart devices.

I think there are a couple more but I honestly can’t remember them all and I’m still writing stream of consciousness so I don’t want to stop to look it up. It has been an incredibly crazy few weeks as just the smallest “launch” of this tech stack has generated more buzz than I can possibly process without a lot more coffee and a lot less sleep.

Good think I’ve got some good people starting to come together around me. Amazing project managers and business analysts, salespeople, and additional developers. Even some established entrepreneurs if I am not mistaken.

I would be more than happy to also look at a licensing model with other SIs. If you are an SI looking to add some immediate and HIGH value to your clients (potentially in the face of salesforce’s reference architecture) please email me. (greg@cloudpremise.com) I am working on both a referral agreement as well as a business provider agreement for other companies to implement Reactforce without me or my teams help.

And this software is as cool as I know it is. I resurrected my last failed start-up into this software and it is honestly just a pleasure to use it in the face of such slow and clunky enterprise software and distributed platforms.

I wonder if that old CEO would be interested in having equity in MY company someday. God knows i’ve missed enough sleep to someday earn a nice vacation… and this product has legs to get to $100M (or more?) (unlike his company did to be honest).

Thanks for reading… hopefully next time you hear from some if not all of the above projects will be off to the races.

I feel like Prometheus handing down the fire from the heavens or Obi-wan Kenobi handing you a lightsaber. I hope you will take it. I know it is scary.

Greg
