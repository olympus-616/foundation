Beta Launch!

When I started this blog I literally wanted to just document the technical process of how to build an app on Salesforce. I honestly didn’t know which app would be built because the technology stack is so powerful and flexible I wasn’t quite sure which direction it would take.

First of all I want to say that I have an incredible development team that works with me on these Reactforce projects, among others. I would not be remotely capable of building anything at this point without them. Therefore my blog has started to migrate from very hands on technical engineer to very busy tech founder trying to figure out which shiny object to focus on next.

I am so excited to cross the chasm from consulting to product development again. About 8 years ago I had some success with my first Salesforce ISV and since that company exited to a MarTech out of Silicon Valley I’ve been more obsessed than Prometheus to return to my creative roots. Consulting pays the bills, but crafting something that you love is a deep desire based at the roots of many of our (all of our?) DNA.

Well its finally here… The Reactforce Enterprise Portal is now in Beta. I don’t imagine I will be successful taking the term “Reactforce” to market due to potential trademark infringement with both Salesforce and Meta. Not two companies I particularly want to be my enemies. Ok… if I can’t call it Reactforce I will call it “Incredibly beautiful, fast, secure, and robust full stack applications in a box, written in React and all hosted on Salesforce”. That doesn’t sound that good though… so for now I will stick with Reactforce.

The beta is now available if you would like to check it out. You can reach out to me directly at greg@cloudpremise.com or on my linkedin: https://www.linkedin.com/in/gregheartcloud/

What does it mean to be a beta customer for a Reactforce portal:

That really depends on your business. This technology scales from a mom & pop shop to the largest fortune 100’s you can imagine. Because I am selling a SOLUTION. A solution consists of multiple parts, and the crown jewel is finally ready for preview.

Because I am bootstrapping this thing (for now) I don’t have a huge amount of time to build marketing materials, but I promise they are coming. Just way behind the technology. Hey… maybe I’ll ask Chat-GPT to do write some more articles for me.

As you can see, the use cases are all over the place. But the technology has passed the test and the customers continue to increase. The market has validated the software which is extremely exciting… and now we move into the next phase which is how to spread the wealth and knowledge of this technology solution to others. There is only so many hours in my day and I have already saved my clients millions if not hundreds of millions of dollars over time and total cost of ownership.

My team and I inventoried each serverless capability we have successfully deployed in the past few years. Most if not all of them are in our latest product, or will be. The possibilities of this platform are very far reaching because it is both an architecture as well as a pre-defined solution for building serverless systems, largely based upon Salesforce.com as the primary database.

Here is the capabilities matrix…. I wonder why I can’t get my documentation project done… the technology is somewhat self documenting (to me and my team anyway) and so now we are turning our attention to the documentation for others to build and implement Reactforce projects on their own.

Here are the building blocks we have used to build these portals:

  1. Enterprise Backend (Salesforce Managed Package) which exposes a lot of the following concepts to a customer org
  2. Enterprise Portal (Salesforce Managed Package and Reactforce portal administration system).
  3. API Manager — the entire backend architecture is API first design and we include the necessary tools and infrastructure to stand up completely custom, Rest based API with a few clicks. All running from within Salesforce. Internal and external applications can use the API which will help you to organize the business functions of your technology stack in a meaningful and scalable way. (Mulesoft not required)
  4. Content Manager (in alpha) — Dynamically modify your static content on a Reactforce site. Give content management controls to your business users to manage your internal and external static content. Also supports translations!
  5. IdP — Pixel Perfect custom sign up and log-in screens to help you through any of your authentication use cases for your Reactforce portal. Utilize licensed users of Salesforce, or a completely separate User store such as Azure Active Directory, AWS Cognito, Auth0, or even simple email verification.
  6. CMDB (in alpha) — Manage your companies configuration using best-in-class ITIL processes and standards.
  7. Marketing Attribution — Track website performance or any other attributable activity to determine key indicators such as velocity, cycle-time, conversion-rates, and real-time ROI from external advertising providers. This is an even better version of the app that is now somewhere lost in the halls of silicon valley.
  8. Analytics Integration — We surface key indicators directly through the React portals through Salesforce, Google and Facebook data sets.
  9. React sites, apps, or portals that deploy almost anywhere such as Salesforce LWC, Visualforce, Force.com sites, Digital Communities, Page Layouts, Lightning Tabs, utility bars… pretty much anywhere you want.
  10. Dynamic landing pages built and customized in React that use Salesforce configuration and security rules to build out your dynamic forms on the fly.
  11. Native iOS — we have adapters for building Swift apps that hook into Reactforce backends fast and easy.
  12. React Native — our preference on the mobile side… build a cross-platform mobile app as an extension of your Reactforce backend implementation.
  13. Pre-built integrations with SendGrid, AWS SES, Mailgun, Twilio, and others. Send and receive unlimited emails and texts directly from Salesforce. Stop worrying about governor limits or licensing rules.
  14. Web chat (in alpha) — Stand up a Chat-GPT or human based chatbot on your website in minutes.
  15. Build custom integrations using your existing knowledge of Salesforce APEX that plug seamlessly and immediately into the Reactforce front-end.
  16. For particular customers we have moved workflow from Salesforce into AWS and have both Lambda and SQS work queues ready to go should you need them.
  17. Advanced Case Management for managing SLA based, configuration defined, self-reporting queues. A necessity for any large call center or data processing operation.
  18. We have prebuilt solutions for managing Documents, Tasks, and Notifications with your Reactforce portal users, regardless of their licensing model.
  19. Build forms on the fly using SObjects.
  20. Build dynamic data storage objects directly from React using what we affectionally refer to as “RfObjects”. Each app can manage its entire application using one custom SObject!
  21. Configuration management. Manage, change, and encrypt platform secrets and configuration variables for managing application settings and secrets.
  22. Specialty libraries for managing all of the nuance of Salesforce queries, DML, and SOSL based upon 15 years of Salesforce experience.
  23. Async work management framework. Define generic work types that can be executed in queue-able, batch, or future processes. Schedule, unschedule, pause, resume, and log against all of your processes.
  24. Data versioning — snapshot your data at each and every change, not limited to Salesforce History tracking feature. Roll-back to earlier versions.
  25. Track, monitor, and alert on Salesforce limits or other custom metrics
  26. Easily integrate with DynamoDB and Firebase databases using cross-origin API calls from React or server side api calls with our Apex adapters. We will be adding support for both Hubspot and MongoDb as well.
  27. Establish and authenticate your identity with third party identity providers including Salesforce, Cognito, Auth0, Microsoft, Google, Facebook, and coming soon, Apple!
  28. Social engagement tracking with facebook, instagram, and google
  29. Host the Reactforce app from Salesforce, or migrate it to AWS, Azure, or Netlify. We will be adding support for Hubspot this year!
  30. Meet all of your security and regulation requirements with data that never has to leave Salesforce. Your data is encrypted end-to-end in transit and even can be encrypted at rest if desired.
  31. Temporarily or permanently grant elevated permissions in your salesforce org or Reactforce apps. Report against any and all security changes for audits or forensic analysis.
  32. Define and execute a data retention strategy by automatically purging (or marking for delete) data within your environment. Automatically prune log files. Systematically delete old and irrelevant data.
  33. Host the solution within your own Salesforce org
  34. Host the solution from your own CDN.
  35. Sign up with us as a SaaS customer and we will even host the Salesforce and other underlining cloud providers for you. LITERALLY ZERO TOUCH, TURN KEY SOLUTIONS.
  36. Use our development tools to immediately provision Reactforce template apps or other components of the stack.
  37. Use our SF quick start to be up and running with react running natively on Salesforce.
  38. Use our Netlify Quick start to be up and running fast with your own automatically updating serverless Reactforce portal.
  39. We can setup a serverless CI/CD pipeline for all of your Reactforce development, whether its front end or backend! Just write code, approve the PR, and watch the magic happen.
  40. Your system is fully backed up and we can even manage that part for you as well.
  41. Your technology is fully monitored using a combination of front-end and back-end techniques. We feed this into our proprietary ELK stack. Someday we plan to expose the ELK stack as a service to our customers.
  42. We have 24x7 support available, depending on the size of your site of course.
  43. Let us guide you through the best practices of deploying Reactforce portals using ITIL processes, ALM applications, and other processes that will help you to obtain HIPAA, SOCII, ISO27001, PCI, and any other compliance you are looking for.
  44. Use our tools, or even our team, to help you craft your next software creation.
