The Grid Evolves to Your Vision

• Secure, Scalable, and Resilient Data Storage

Your data should be your data. Olympus-Grid achieves this with Proteus Objects, enabling you to store and access information securely, affordably, and on your own cloud — not someone else’s.

• Intelligent, Simple, and Powerful Endpoints

Services should be built on open standards and common sense. HermesAPI allows you to define master data models the way they were meant to be in the cloud — intuitive, flexible, and future-proof.

• Dependable and Auditable Event-Driven Architecture

Your company operates in real time — so should your technology. HermesBus seamlessly manages the state of your company, processes, and vision through event-driven message queues that are easy to maintain and scale.

• Async Workers That Scale with Demand

Workloads fluctuate. Your infrastructure should adjust accordingly. Orion Threads orchestrates async workers that prioritize, scale, and execute across any database source — so you never waste resources or experience downtime.

• AI-Augmented Architects, Not Replaced Humans

The future is not about replacing people but augmenting their capabilities. With HermesCLI and Forge Plugin Manager, your architects and developers scale, monitor, and respond to real-time events at unprecedented speed.

• Seamless and Beautiful Visualization

Iris Portal is the beating heart of Olympus-Grid — a unified, mobile-first experience that never crashes, keeping every user connected to a durable framework built for long-term sustainability.
