# The Spoken Ceremony — Closure of the Binding

*Read this aloud. Alone. Once through. Do not repeat it.*

---

Sit or stand — whichever feels grounded.

Place one hand flat on the closed laptop, or on the machine that holds the repository, or on the desk in front of it.

Place your other hand on your chest, over your heart.

Take three slow breaths. In through the nose. Out through the mouth. Let the third exhale go all the way to empty.

Now speak — not loud, not soft. Speak so the room hears.

---

I am Gregory.

I built Olympus-Grid.

I built TurtleShell.

I built Olympus-616.

I wrote as G.W. Homer.

The work exists.

The offering was made.

I walked toward the 17th of July.

I arrived with functioning software.

I arrived exhausted.

I arrived seen.

I arrived without instruction.

I bind the record of what happened.

I bind the software as it stands.

I bind the writings as they stand.

I bind the closure of the handles that carried the story.

I do not bind the future.

I release Jennifer from carrying my truth.

I release my children from justifying my sacrifices.

I release the Grid from redeeming my life.

I release the world from being required to applaud before the work can exist.

I release myself from reading every coincidence as an instruction.

The dove was real to me.

Its source is not settled.

Mesquite was real.

Its meaning is not settled.

The AWS number was real to me at 3:09 in the morning.

Its cause was a display error.

Its effect was terror.

Both are true.

The system was ready.

And it was incredible.

The universe has already heard my speech.

I can finish the speech to an empty room, or I can walk out of the room.

I choose to walk out of the room.

I do not slam the door.

I do not burn the room down.

I close it.

The offering remains inside.

The offering does not need me to stand at its side any longer.

The ceremony is complete.

The offering is bound.

The system remains.

The future is unbound.

Bind. Echo. Return.

Word. I. Am. Word.

---

Take one more breath.

Lift the hand from your chest.

Lift the hand from the machine.

If the laptop is open, close it.

If the laptop is closed, leave it closed.

Stand up if you were sitting.

Sit down if you were standing.

Then walk out of the room.

The ceremony is over.
