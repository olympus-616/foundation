I Came, I Saw, I Conquered

By gregheartcloud

There are moments in a builder’s life when you realize the battle wasn’t just uphill — the mountain you climbed didn’t even exist until you built it under your own feet. For me, that mountain is called Olympus-Grid.

And sitting at the summit of it now, is Iris Portal — the crown jewel of a backend architecture that shouldn’t be possible, let alone already running in production.

What Is Iris Portal?

It’s the front door to every product I’ve ever dreamed of building — and a few that dreamed of me.

It’s a multi-tenant, domain-routed, namespace-aware, microfrontend delivery engine that sits entirely inside Salesforce. I’m routing multiple domains, across multiple sub-brands, using a single Salesforce license and one production node. Static resource bundles resolve by namespace. Config is declarative. Maintenance banners are dynamic. It scales like something built on AWS, but runs like it’s stitched into the platform itself.

What Does That Mean?

It means that what used to take separate orgs, separate teams, and six figures in cloud ops now takes one config file and one license.

  • Support portal? Routed.
  • App portal? Routed.
  • Marketing site? Routed.
  • Future product deployments? Handled.
  • Theme switching? Isolated.
  • Namespace collisions? Avoided.

One customer could save hundreds of thousands of dollars in infrastructure and licensing costs. Now scale that to 10. Or 100. Or 10,000.

And that’s just with Iris Portal running on the surface.

And Then There’s Olympus-GPT

The AI layer isn’t theoretical. It’s embedded. Real GPT routing, logic injection, portal-aware context modeling — and it’s only just begun.

I’ve given my system a voice.

Soon, I’ll give it to others.

Final Thoughts

One time someone said they thought I was manic and I was highly offended.

It took a few more months than I had anticipated to prove to myself just how much more “manic” I even was.

I can’t wait to see what happens when I show people how they can speak software to life.

My first app will be:

“I’m so manic I need to create a surf training application.”

See you around Medium.
