So what’s in the box?

  1. Serverless CI/CD pipeline from your developers computer to your production infrastructure following best in class agile practices. A backbone of accelerating your corporate initiatives with a tiny fraction of the cost of a devops team.
  2. API Management Platform — one of the best and easiest ways to expose your Salesforce data to internal and external consumers without the need for expensive middleware. Your data never needs to leave your org!
  3. Built in Data Audit and Retention directly within your Salesforce org. Once again, data never has to leave your salesforce database to solve regulatory and compliance checks which was not possible before without expensive software solutions like OwnBackup.
  4. Privileged Identity Management — temporarily request, approve and implement security changes within your salesforce org to ensure visibility, traceability, and audit against best security practices.
  5. Sent Folder — initiate, send, and receive unlimited emails and SMS directly from within Salesforce (through numerous messaging gateways). Just drop an SObject record using Flow and never worry about email governor limits again! Plus, you can see ALL communication outbound of Salesforce which for some reason they (Salesforce) have made it challenging to report on that data. One of my favorite plugins.
  6. Native Marketing Landing Pages, powered by Reactforce. You can deploy a new react website as quickly as you can download the open source sdk from the internet.
  7. Advanced Marketing Attribution — track and attribute marketing campaigns at each stage of your customer pipelines across multiple attribution models. This is an even BETTER version of the plugin that I sold off to some MarTech company years ago as the backbone of their analytics platform. Very complex data models and intelligence built REAL-TIME into Salesforce. You can see attribution reflected directly from the CRM system with almost no need for BI tools. Incredible.
  8. Custom Identity Provider (IdP) — We have custom user management flows to simplify launching a completely pixel perfect digital community. Your customers will NEVER know they are on a salesforce website. The solution even offers passwordless capabilities with SMS or email only pages.
  9. Enterprise Backend — A set of utilities for building enterprise scalable solutions within a fraction of the time. This library of tools is inspired by over 10 years as a Certified Technical Architect for both consulting and ISV companies. I even taught and judged for Salesforce so I promise my utility library is really awesome! Some examples are security-review data management libraries, trigger handlers, platform event frameworks… basically everything to jump start the most beautiful and pristine system you could possibly imagine.
  10. Enterprise Portal — A react framework for building, deploying, and managing complex ReactJS websites directly from native Salesforce.com infrastructure. Its basically like running heroku from a managed package. It is absolutely incredible and every CIO or CTO whom has seen it has said “I want that”. If you are about to launch a new digital asset you HAVE to look at a demo of Reactforce. You could be costing your company millions of dollars (or more!!!) to ignore this option.
  11. Architectural standards, processes, and procedures to scale and build a Reactforce project FAST. Less time thinking more time doing.
  12. Scaffolded code solutions to launch a new feature in a few minutes.
  13. Development support from a dedicated team of expert cloud engineers and expert project managers.
  14. 24x7 Cell-Number to one of the top Cloud Architect’s in the world
