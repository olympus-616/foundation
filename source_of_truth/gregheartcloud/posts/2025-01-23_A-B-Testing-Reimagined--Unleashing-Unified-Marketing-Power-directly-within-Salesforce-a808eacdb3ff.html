The Traditional A/B Testing Problem

Most companies turn to external platforms like Marketo, HubSpot, or Marketing Cloud for A/B testing, often incurring significant expenses. While effective, these tools come with limitations:

  1. Costly Licensing Fees: Marketing automation platforms charge steep fees, often priced per user or per feature.
  2. Integration Complexities: Connecting these systems to existing CRM platforms like Salesforce can be a lengthy and error-prone process.
  3. Delayed Insights: External tools often rely on batch data processing, which delays access to actionable insights.
  4. Scalability Issues: Managing high traffic volumes or simultaneous tests can push external tools to their limits, resulting in downtime or incomplete reporting.

The result? Many organizations find themselves spending millions on tools that are neither agile nor tailored to their specific needs.
