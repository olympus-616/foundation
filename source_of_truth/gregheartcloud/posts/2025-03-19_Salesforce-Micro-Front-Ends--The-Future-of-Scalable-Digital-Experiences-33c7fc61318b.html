What Are Micro Front-Ends?

A Micro Front-End (MFE) is an architectural style where individual pieces of an application’s front-end are developed, deployed, and scaled independently.

In the same way that microservices revolutionized back-end development by breaking monolithic architectures into smaller, independently deployable services, Micro Front-Ends apply the same principle to the UI layer.

The Problem with Traditional Front-Ends

  • Monolithic UIs are fragile — A small update to a large React or Angular application can break the entire experience.
  • Scaling is hard — Large applications require massive infrastructure, complex caching, and heavy DevOps pipelines.
  • A/B testing and feature rollouts are slow — Business teams need weeks or months to test new UI changes.

The Power of Micro Front-Ends

With Micro Front-Ends, different teams can:

✅ Develop and deploy independent UI components without affecting the whole app.
 ✅ Enable A/B testing and feature rollouts instantly at subdomain, IP, or user-segment levels.
 ✅ Reduce infrastructure costs by only running what is needed.
