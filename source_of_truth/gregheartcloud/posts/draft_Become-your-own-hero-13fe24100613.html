Become your own hero

I have become increasingly anxious since finally allowing myself to take in how far along AI had come along. I thought if I closed out my mind to it some how it would just go away. La La La La I can’t hear you progress! I can’t hear you future!!!

I finally let it in. The future is coming. And its coming fast. Faster every day. While metaphyically, spiritually, or pharmacutically we are all trying to relax and live in the moment. Love one another. Forget that there are wars going on. Forget that there are people out there’s who’s best days wouldn’t rival your worst day. Forget that our government just had an insurrection. Forget that you are unable to speak your truth to anyone who doesn’t agree with it.

I finally cast aside the part of me that cares about whatever people think. It was when I started being super genuinely honest with people that my life started to shift for a better tomorrow. Not that I was a dishonest. Far from it. But I wasn’t genuine. Maybe more of a people pleaser for LOTs of reasons that I have ruled as outside of scope for this blog…

I’m pretty sure it was when I told a customer I could build them a product for $45M that they had just built for $100M but didn’t work.

It was like lightning from the heavens striking down to me… teaching me about integrity, value, and purpose.

What did the hospital want?

A website. It did some things. It made life easier for some people. It helped things to go smoother.

Guess what? At the end of the $100M it didn’t even do all the things they asked for. It still required swivel chairs and manual data entry. Um… I’m pretty sure I could build a working lightsaber if I had $100m. I’m sure a marketing front end of a hospital registration system can be completed for a measly $45M.

I’ve been a serial entrepreneur since I took “Visionary Leadership” at the University of Colorado Denver about 20 years ago. But I already had a wife, a dog, a mortgage, and kids on the way. This was going to take some time to turn this ship….

I’ve built these systems. By hand.

I’ve managed these projects.

I’ve configured these systems.

I’ve customized these platforms.

…

And for what…

…

It was all leading up to me racing against the machines of AI to build valuable software that helps companies to complete their mission. Without paying ridiculous amounts in license and on-going support.

So what is the point? If AI is going to make everything obsolete, then we are ALL in a race to find a chair before the music stops.

Isn’t my whole software architecture basically a blueprint for how Chat-GPT could render you a HIPAA compliate, 99.999% uptime portal in about 5 minutes…. I think I maybe helping the machines not “racing” them.

I did not intend to take on a persona
