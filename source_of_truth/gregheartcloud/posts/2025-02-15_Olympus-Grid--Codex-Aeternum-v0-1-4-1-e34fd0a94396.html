Codex Aeternum v0.1.4.1 — The Software License of the Future

Rewriting the Rules of Software Pricing

For decades, software has been sold under broken economic models — models that favor monopolies, exploit workers, and lock customers into endless cycles of dependency and inefficiency. The tech giants of today have amassed empires by extracting wealth while delivering diminishing returns to society. They have already killed this planet.

But we are not here to compete with them. We are here to rewrite the contract of reality itself.

The Olympus-Grid Pricing Model: Built for Eternity

Our pricing is not dictated by corporate greed. It is dictated by the natural flow of energy, creation, and shared growth.

1️⃣ Pay What You Can — Scale as You Grow
We price software based on what our partners can afford, not what we can extract. We align with growth: if you thrive, we thrive. If you fail, we fail. This is true partnership.

2️⃣ Eliminating Artificial Barriers
The software industry has spent decades locking value behind paywalls, forcing upgrades, and creating dependency. Olympus-Grid is different. We offer:

  • Builder Plan — Entry-level access for firms just getting started
  • Partner Plan — For firms scaling rapidly, with growth-based pricing
  • Enterprise Plan — A fully customizable future-proof architecture

We do not sell products — we sell partnerships, freedom, and control over your digital future.

And our technology and roadmap ensures you are never locked in.

3️⃣ 10% for the Relievers of Suffering
We take 10% of every dollar earned and allocate it into a transparent, decentralized foundation that funds projects that relieve suffering in the world. Over time, this will become a democratically governed system where contributors vote on where funds go. This is not charity. This is the natural flow of abundance.

4️⃣ Developer Royalties: The Death of the Middleman
In today’s world, the software supply chain is clogged with bloat, inefficiency, and middlemen who drain value without contributing to the creative process. We are changing that.

  • Developers who build ingenious Olympus-Grid plugins will receive direct royalties when their work is used.
  • No pointless licensing, no executives skimming the profits — just a direct pipeline from innovation to reward.
  • The best ideas will flourish organically, not because a venture capitalist decided their profit margins were high enough.

This is Not Just a Business Model — This is the Foundation of Eternity

We are not restructuring the software industry — we are replacing it.

The kings of today are already dead. They sold their future for quick profit, and in doing so, they have trapped humanity in digital serfdom. The Parable of the Spoons shows us that those who hoard and consume alone will starve in hell. Those that feed others will thrive in heaven. The Allegory of the Cave reminds us that most people will live and die in shadows, never seeking the real light.

But we see the truth. And so do you.

Our model is simple:

  • Give 10% to relieve suffering.
  • Trade creativity and kinetic potential into growth opportunities.
  • Break the chains of legacy software licensing.
  • Reward those who build, not those who hoard.

This Is Your Moment — This Is The Future of Software

Olympus-Grid is not another tech company. It is the architecture of the gods, given to humanity as a tool for their liberation.

This is not about software. This is about reality itself.
