After more than a decade of relentless dedication and three years of meticulous development, Olympus-Grid is poised for its imminent release to general availability. A Platform Designed for Excellence

Olympus-Grid is a fully native Salesforce managed package, combining the power of modern cloud architecture with seamless Salesforce integration. Whether you’re building internal tools or customer-facing solutions, Olympus-Grid is engineered to support your ambitions.

Key Features of Olympus-Grid

1. Pixel-Perfect React Applications

Host fully responsive, automatically scaled, React-routing applications that integrate seamlessly with your Salesforce data. With no web-server infrastructure required, Olympus-Grid empowers developers to create visually stunning, high-performance front ends without compromise or complexity.

2. World-Class API Management

Rivaling platforms like MuleSoft or Express.js, Olympus-Grid makes it effortless to expose custom APIs written in Apex or Flow, ensuring rapid deployment and secure data management.

3. Enhanced Salesforce Capabilities

Fill Salesforce’s critical gaps with features like fully logged platform events, traceable workflows, dead letter queues, and auto-retry capabilities — all built natively into the platform.

4. Asynchronous Work Management

Write, deploy, manage, track, schedule, pause, and run jobs on demand with a streamlined, intuitive interface. Olympus-Grid simplifies asynchronous processes, allowing your team to focus on delivering results.

5. Advanced Plugin Management

Extend and customize the platform with ease using our advanced plugin system. Protect your intellectual property with encrypted license keys while enabling your organization — or ISVs — to innovate securely.

6. Dynamic Object Integration

Enable your React applications to write to generic APIs, while the backend stores data in Salesforce, Google Firebase, Azure Cosmos, or AWS DynamoDB. This feature is the foundation of our forthcoming data archive product, which ensures your apps remain data-agnostic and scalable.

7. Enterprise-Ready Identity Management

Issue secure JWT tokens for Salesforce users, or extend authentication to external users through secure email links or passwordless SMS logins. Olympus-Grid’s native IDP ensures security and flexibility.

Power Meets Simplicity

Olympus-Grid is designed to make enterprise application development faster, more cost-effective, and accessible to teams of all sizes. With serverless CI/CD pipelines, you can go from concept to production-ready with a fraction of the time and resources typically required.
