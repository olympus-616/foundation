The Product is Velocity

The disillusionment I felt watching all of our modern-day Caesar’s launch themselves into outerspace was really something. I don’t know if one other single moment in my life has ever left me feeling more hopeless for our society (don’t worry… our planet will be just fine after a few weeks of our extinction).

What are we doing here?

What are our goals?

Without getting lost in philosophy I will try to dial it back a bit. But seriously… why am I working around the clock to grow my business… so that someday I can launch myself further into outer space? That seems to be the collective goal for our technology and therefore our planetary leaders. Their technology tools train us to sit and do nothing other than to consume their information. In exchange our tech giants get bigger, richer, and more powerful. Impressive!

Erin Brockovich: Twenty-eight Billion Dollars
Wow!!

Last time I checked we are having multiple world wars. There still are places in the world needing clean water. We haven’t recovered from the culture shock of global pandemics and lock-downs. The price of food has doubled. I wouldn’t say this is the golden-era of prosperity.

But instead we seem to be inside another technology revolution! Wow! AI! now the technology can take even MORE responsibility for our lives! Now we can think even less! We can do even less! We can scroll our 24x7 personalized advertising system (btw I am referring to social media). Do you have a problem? Ask the technology! Do you have an errand? Ask the technology! Do you need to use your cognitive thinking skills? Ask the technology!

I am most likely in the second half of my life. My mind has changed from the how to the why. I try to keep myself in the how but it is much easier to delegate that to employees.

I am a little frustrated that I have to raise my children in an age where not only do we have to worry about school shootings, but we also have to worry about our kids LITERALLY being plugged into technology not unlike some of my favorite sci-fi movies.

So what are we doing? What are we working for? What are we building? What are we trying for?

And then it hit me.

We are working for velocity. We are working to increase velocity in our own lives through the paying down of debt and the speed to which we can attain the (on average) 65-year-old targeted retirement age.

We are selling velocity to our b2b customers. We are basically selling them software, services, or our time — to increase their velocity of revenue acquisition.

We are selling velocity to our b2c consumers. Don’t want to wait 2 days for your amazon prime? Now you can wait 1 day! 1 day too long? Now we can send drones out to you immediately! We must go faster!

Faster, faster, FASTER!

Question… Where are we going?

I guess the neat and unique and often challenging aspect of our species is that we are all going on different journeys. We are all going somewhere different from each other. But what we seem to share as a common virtue between most people, is that we want to get there as soon as possible.

I’m done with the existential dread lead up…

If you want to go faster as a business I highly recommend you contact us regarding our technology services and solutions. We have unlocked the magic of Salesforce and Cloud technology that is hundreds if not thousands of times faster than that of what you thought was achievable. So you can get back to going whereever it is that you are going.

Until then I will be working as hard and fast as I can to retire so that I can do nothing all day too.
